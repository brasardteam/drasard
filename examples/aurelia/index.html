Our story

A dining room with a sense of place.

Aurelia was imagined as a quiet counterpoint to the city: warm timber, soft light and a kitchen that treats Latvia's landscape as its starting point. Every detail is deliberate, from the ceramics on the table to the final pour of the evening.

The result is a restaurant concept built for intimate dinners, celebrations, business meals and slow evenings that deserve a little more attention.
