The North Table way

Simple food.
Deep flavour.

North Table brings the character of the Baltic into a relaxed neighbourhood setting. The menu follows the seasons, the kitchen follows the fire, and the room is designed for long evenings.

The concept is deliberately flexible: lunch can be quick and generous, dinner can stretch for three hours, and the private table can turn into a celebration.

01 / LOCAL

From nearby fields.

Vegetables, grains, fish and carefully sourced meat from Latvian producers and makers.

02 / FIRE

Cooked with character.

Open-flame cooking gives simple ingredients depth, texture and a little smoke.

03 / SHARED

Made to stay awhile.

Generous plates, natural wine and a room that feels as good at 20:00 as it does at 22:30.
