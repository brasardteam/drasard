The Ember experience

Smoke.
Char.
Season.

Ember is built around the oldest cooking method and a very modern idea of hospitality. The kitchen works over flame, the wine list leans natural, and the room gets louder as the evening goes on.

The concept is designed for a full evening: an aperitif at the bar, small plates by the fire, a generous main course and something sweet before the last glass.

01 / FIRE

Everything touches flame.

Coal, ember and smoke bring depth to carefully sourced ingredients.

02 / WINE

Stay for the late hours.

Dinner starts at six. The energy carries well beyond the last plate.
