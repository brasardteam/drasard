The experience

Ember is a small room with one big idea. Everything that reaches your table has passed through fire.

The grill is the centre of the room. Charcoal made from local birch burns for hours before service, and the cooks tend it like a living thing: raising it for a hard sear, banking it for a slow roast, burying a whole beetroot in the ash overnight.

Photograph: ash-baked beetroot on the coals

Smoke is an ingredient here. A little in the butter, a little in the cream, a lot in the fish. We cook seasonal produce from small Latvian farms, and we cook it simply, so that charcoal and ingredient can speak for themselves.

The room is low-lit and close, with thirty-four seats around the grill and along the wall. Dinner starts at five and runs late. On Friday and Saturday, the kitchen and the bar stay open until two in the morning.

Wine

Bright, savoury and made by hand.

Fire loves acidity, texture and a little age. Our list favours small growers and cool climates: mineral whites, skin-contact oranges, reds with lift. We pour thirty by the glass and keep a cellar of two hundred bottles.

Not sure what to open? Ask the team. They will pick something unexpected, and then tell you why it works.
