Modern Baltic dining, quietly reimagined.

An intimate dining room in Riga where Baltic ingredients meet contemporary technique.

Reserve a tableExplore the menu

Twenty-two seats, one kitchen, a long view of the season.

Aurelia is a small restaurant with a single idea: cook the Baltic as it is now, with care and restraint.

Photograph: the pass
Photograph: hands, rye, butter

Our ingredients come from a short list of Latvian and Estonian growers, fishers and foragers. Trout from clear northern rivers, beetroot pulled in June, juniper and pine from the Kurzeme coast. We cook with the seasons, so the menu moves quietly each week.

The technique is contemporary. Fermentation, smoke, long cures and precise heat give familiar flavours a new clarity. The cooking is never loud. A plate should taste of exactly what it is.

The room is intimate by design. Twenty-two seats, a low sound and a team that knows when to speak and when to step back. Our sommelier builds a cellar around small growers, from the Loire to Tokaj, with a particular love of the cool-climate wines of the north.

Wine, chosen slowly.

Our list is built around growers who farm with care and bottle with restraint. Expect mineral whites, lightly textured oranges and reds with lift rather than weight, many from small estates you may not know yet.

A wine pairing is available with the tasting menu, and our sommelier is always happy to suggest a single glass or a bottle to share.
