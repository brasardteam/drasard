The wine pairing

Six pours, chosen to follow the menu rather than lead it.

Expect a cool-climate Riesling beside the trout, a light red from the Jura with the duck and a late-harvest Tokaji with dessert. A non-alcoholic pairing of ferments, infusions and juices is available for €60 per guest.

Chef's table

Two seats at the pass. Watch the kitchen work and receive each course from the chef.

The chef's table runs the full tasting with two additional bites, a conversation with the kitchen and a glass of champagne on arrival. Available Wednesday to Saturday, one sitting a night.

Vegetarian

A full vegetarian tasting menu is available with 48 hours' notice. Vegan menus can be arranged with a little more notice.

Allergies

Please tell us about any allergy when you book. Our kitchen handles gluten, dairy, nuts, fish, shellfish and celery every day.

Children

We welcome guests aged 12 and over. Please speak to us if you would like to bring a younger guest.
