Nova was created for slow mornings, quick lunches and everything in between.

We started with a simple idea: a café that is as good at 07:30 with a flat white as it is at 18:00 with a glass of natural wine. Coffee comes from a small Riga roaster. Pastry is baked each morning in our own kitchen. The menu follows the seasons, and the room is built for sitting.

We are a neighbourhood place first. Regulars have a table, newcomers get a smile, and nobody is hurried out the door.

Specialty coffeeRoasted in Riga, brewed to order
Fresh bakeryBaked on site, every morning
Seasonal ingredientsLatvian farms where we can
Relaxed atmosphereLaptops, books and long catch-ups
