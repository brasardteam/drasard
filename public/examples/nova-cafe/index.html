01 - Our story

Roasted under our own roof, poured with intent.

Nova began in 2019 with a secondhand 12-kilo roaster we named Hilda, a borrowed counter, and the suspicion that a city this loud deserved a quieter cup.

Five years on, Hilda still runs first crack every morning at 06:45. We buy from farms we have actually visited, roast in batches small enough to argue over, and dial in until the sweetness shows up on its own - no syrups, no shortcuts.

The room itself is built for lingering: fourteen seats, long mornings, records on heavy rotation. Ask for the wifi password only if you truly must leave.
