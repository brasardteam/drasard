Our story

A café for the people who live around it.

Photograph: opening morning

It started with a flat white.

We wanted a place where the coffee is excellent, the food is honest and nobody minds if you stay for three hours.

Nova opened on Brīvības iela in the spring, in a former print shop with tall windows and a floor that has seen better days. We kept the floor. We kept the light. We added a long bench, a very good espresso machine and an oven that hasn't been switched off since.

The kitchen is small by design. Our baker starts before dawn, the coffee comes from a roaster ten minutes away, and the lunch menu is written around what the market has that week. When something is at its best, we cook it simply and put it on a plate.

How we work.

Coffee first

One house blend, one rotating single origin, always dialled in by hand.

Baked here

Pastry, bread and cake made in our kitchen, every morning.

Local where it counts

Dairy, vegetables and eggs from Latvian producers within two hours of Riga.

Come as you are.

Bring a laptop, a book or a friend. We have sockets, daylight and a stack of board games for rainy Sundays. Dogs are welcome on the terrace and prams are welcome everywhere.
